# Credits & Asset Provenance

The **Jalura Framework** is committed to clean rights, transparent provenance, and permissive licensing.

---

## 💻 Code & Architecture
- **Framework Development:** JaluraDEV
- **License:** [MIT License](LICENSE)
- **Engine:** Built with and for the [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org/) (Godot 4.3+)

---

## 🎨 3D Models & Environment Assets
- **Showcase 3D Models:**
  - Original low-poly 3D models (harbor props, ship cabin diorama, platforms, NPCs, and items) created using generative AI tools (Tripo3D / Blender) and procedural Godot primitives under permissive, copyright-free terms.
  - All character and object meshes are original designs and contain no copyrighted characters, third-party trademarks, or proprietary game rips.
- **Embedded Textures & Materials:**
  - Standard PBR materials configured directly inside Godot with low-overhead embedded textures.

---

## 🔊 Audio Subsystem
- **Architecture:** Pre-configured 3D audio positional players with surface-aware footstep detection (grass, wood, stone) and action audio hooks.
- **Asset Slots:** Sound effect streams are intentionally left empty by default to prevent repository bloat and ensure zero licensing ambiguity. Users can freely assign their own `.wav` or `.ogg` sound assets in the Inspector.

---

## 🖼️ User Interface & Icons
- **UI Themes:** Godot 4 StyleBoxes and custom vector themes.
- **Icons & Visuals:** Original logos and vector iconography created for the Jalura project.
